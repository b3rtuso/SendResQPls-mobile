## 6. Inter-Agency Coordination and Dispatch Triage

Incident reports submitted through SendResQPls are reviewed in real-time by the MDRRMO Balayan Command Center and triaged based on standardized disaster severity protocols. Depending on the nature of the hazard, alerts and coordinates may be dispatched to:
* **Bureau of Fire Protection (BFP)** – Fire suppression and hazardous chemical response;
* **Philippine National Police (PNP)** – Law enforcement, public order, and vehicular accidents involving injury/crime;
* **Municipal Health Office (MHO)** – Emergency medical services, ambulance routing, and casualty care;
* **Municipal Engineering Office** – Road clearance, bridge structural inspection, and public infrastructure hazards;
* **MDRRMO Rescue Units** – Flash flood extrication, typhoon evacuation, and landslide search-and-rescue.

MDRRMO Balayan dispatchers hold sole operational discretion to prioritize, categorize, reclassify, or dismiss duplicate/unverifiable reports based on on-the-ground intelligence.
