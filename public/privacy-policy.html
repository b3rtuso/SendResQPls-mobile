PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated October 03, 2026

This Privacy Notice for MDRRMO Balayan (doing business as SendResQPls) ("we," "us," or "our"), describes how and why we might access, collect, store, use, and/or share ("process") your personal information when you use our services ("Services"), including when you:
  • Download and use our mobile application (SendResQPls), or any other application of ours that links to this Privacy Notice.
  • Use SendResQPls: A mobile incident reporting app for residents of Balayan, Batangas, enabling them to report disasters and emergencies with photos and location information to MDRRMO Balayan.
  • Engage with us in other related ways, including any customer support, communications, or public safety alerts.
Questions or concerns? Reading this Privacy Notice will help you understand your privacy rights and choices. If you do not agree with our policies and practices, please do not use our Services. If you still have any questions or concerns, please contact us at sendresqpls@gmail.com.

How do you exercise your rights? You may submit a data subject access request or email us at sendresqpls@gmail.com.

1. WHAT INFORMATION DO WE COLLECT?

Personal information you disclose to us

We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us when you register on the Services, express an interest in obtaining information, submit an incident report, or contact us. This may include:

  • Full Name
  • Philippine Mobile Phone Number
  • Verified Email Address
  • Account Authentication Data (Hashed Password)
  • Incident Location Data (GPS Coordinates & Barangay)
  • Incident Photographs & Evidence

Application Data

If you use our application, we may also request access or permission to:

  • Geolocation Information: We request permission to access precise location from your mobile device to attach GPS coordinates to emergency incident reports.
  • Mobile Device Camera & Photo Storage: We request camera and media access so you can take and attach photographs of incident scenes.
  • Push Notifications: We request permission to send push notifications regarding emergency broadcasts, disaster advisories, and status updates on your reported incidents.

Google API Compliance

Our use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

2. HOW DO WE PROCESS YOUR INFORMATION?

We process your information for legitimate public safety and operational purposes, including:

  • Account Creation and Management: To enable login, password resets, and profile management.
  • Emergency Incident Delivery: To route reports to the MDRRMO Balayan Command Center and dispatch emergency teams (fire, police, medical, rescue).
  • User Support and Communication: To answer inquiries and keep citizens informed of report status.
  • System Protection and Fraud Prevention: To prevent false alarms, prank reports, and malicious activity.
  • Legal Compliance: To comply with Philippine disaster risk reduction laws (RA 10121) and statutory mandates.

3. WHEN AND WITH WHOM DO WE SHARE YOUR PERSONAL INFORMATION?

We may share information in specific situations and strictly with the following categories of third parties:

  • Government Entities: MDRRMO Balayan, Bureau of Fire Protection (BFP), Philippine National Police (PNP), and municipal emergency health workers.
  • AI Platforms: Google Cloud AI / Gemini for automated disaster severity analysis of incident photos.
  • Cloud Computing & Data Storage: Cloudinary for image storage, Render for API hosting, and Supabase / PostgreSQL for database records.
  • Communication Tools: Brevo (Sendinblue) for verification/password reset emails, and Firebase Cloud Messaging for push alerts.
  • Google Maps Platform APIs: We use Google Maps for reverse geocoding coordinates. Location data is cached locally on your device to optimize network performance.

Zero Commercial Exploitation: Your personal data is NEVER sold, rented, or leased to advertisers or commercial data brokers.

4. DO WE USE COOKIES AND OTHER TRACKING TECHNOLOGIES?

Our mobile application does not use tracking cookies or marketing beacons. User sessions and preferences are stored locally on the device using HTML5 LocalStorage. Third-party mapping APIs may use technical cookies necessary for rendering map data.

5. DO WE OFFER ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE-BASED PRODUCTS?

Yes. We use Google Cloud AI (Gemini) exclusively for Image Analysis to evaluate incident photographs submitted by users. The AI classifies disaster categories (e.g., fire, flood, vehicular accident) and calculates urgency scores to assist human dispatchers in prioritizing emergency responses. Your data is not used to train public AI models.

6. HOW LONG DO WE KEEP YOUR INFORMATION?

We retain your personal information for as long as you maintain an active account with us. When account deletion is requested, personal identifiers are removed from our active database, subject to municipal emergency audit retention laws.

7. HOW DO WE KEEP YOUR INFORMATION SAFE?

We implement industry-standard technical and organizational security measures, including bcrypt password hashing, TLS encryption for all data in transit, strict role-based access controls, and API rate limiting.

8. WHAT ARE YOUR PRIVACY RIGHTS?

Under the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173), you have the right to access, inspect, rectify, or request deletion of your personal information. You can update your profile directly inside the application settings at any time.

9. CONTROLS FOR DO-NOT-TRACK FEATURES

Because there is currently no universal standard for recognizing Do-Not-Track (DNT) browser signals, our systems do not respond to automated DNT requests.
